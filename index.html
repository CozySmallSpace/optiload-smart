What it does

Answers the questions a plant asks every week

Where are we overloaded, what is late, what can we still fit, and do we need more lines? OptiLoad Smart calculates it from your own production data and shows it in plain screens.

Real load per line, per week

Required shifts against available shifts for every line and week, using your cycle times, OEE, shift pattern, planned downtime and public holidays for each year.

Backlog under control

See overdue work in units, shifts and weeks. Test recovery plans with your own priorities and see what each choice costs the other parts.

How many lines you will need

Set a target utilization and a demand scenario. The Strategic view shows, year by year, the lines needed against the lines installed.

Data checked before it is trusted

Missing cycle times, unassigned volume and parts allocated above 100% are flagged with the exact part numbers, before they distort a plan.

What-if, live

Change OEE, shift length, working days, shifts per day or volumes and watch every screen update. Nothing is written back to your source data.

From weekly plan to daily plan

Pick the parts and quantities for one line and one day. Changeovers and breaks are counted, and you see the load, the finish time and what still fits.

Questions

Good to know

Does it need internet or a cloud account?

No. OptiLoad Smart is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. Your production data stays on your machine.

How many people can use it?

A site license includes up to 3 workstations, which covers the planner, a backup and a manager. Everyone else can receive the PDF reports. Additional workstations can be added.

What data do I need?

Your volume forecast, how volumes are allocated to lines, the cycle time of each part on each line, planned interruptions and public holidays. We provide the template and help you fill it in.

Can it plan more than one year ahead?

Yes. It works with one year or many, with holidays handled per year. The Strategic view shows how many lines each year will require under your target utilization and demand scenario.

How long does it take to start?

Typically about a week from agreement to live on site, depending on how ready your data is. A pilot is a good way to see results before a longer commitment.
